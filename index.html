Why BatStateU

A national university built on engineering.

From a manual training school to the country’s National Engineering University, our work has always been about solving real problems.

The National Engineering University

Declared by Republic Act No. 11694 in 2022 as the first and only National Engineering University in the Philippines.

Recognized quality

A Level IV state university with ISO 9001:2015 certification and internationally accredited engineering and computing programs.

Learning that serves

Instruction, research, and extension services work together so that ideas from our labs reach real communities.
