About us

Leading innovations since 1903.

Our story, our purpose, and the people who shape the university.

History

From a manual training school to a national university.

What began as a small training school for young men has grown with its community, one class and one graduating batch at a time, into the Philippines’ National Engineering University.

  1. 1903

    Established as a Manual Training School, focused on practical skills such as woodworking.

  2. 1905

    Renamed the Batangas Trade School.

  3. 2001

    Converted into Batangas State University by Republic Act No. 9045.

  4. 2022

    Declared The National Engineering University by Republic Act No. 11694.
