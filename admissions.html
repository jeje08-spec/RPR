Process & requirements

Four steps to enroll.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the application form during the admission period and choose your program.

  2. Take the test

    Sit for the university admission test on your scheduled date.

  3. Check results

    Results are released online. Qualified applicants receive enrollment instructions.

  4. Enroll

    Submit your documents, complete your registration, and attend orientation.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who can apply to Batangas State University?

Senior high school graduates, transferees, and returning students who meet the requirements of their chosen program.

Can I choose a second-choice program?

Yes. Applicants can list another program in case the first choice is full or the cut-off score is not met.

Is the university really tuition-free?

Qualified Filipino undergraduates in state universities may be covered by free higher education. Some other fees may still apply.

How do I visit the campus?

Send us a message from the contact page and choose “Book a campus visit.”
